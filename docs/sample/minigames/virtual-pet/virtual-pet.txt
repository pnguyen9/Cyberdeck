[import: story en]

[style: Ending = gold, large, fade-in]

[counter lasting: Hunger = 2]
[counter lasting: Mood = 7]
[counter lasting: Energy = 7]
[counter lasting: Visits = 0]
[counter: Pick = 0]
[decision: Care = Feed | Play | Pet | Nap | Watch | Bye]

[shown: Hunger, Mood, Energy, Visits]

# Pip
[bg: room]
[music: stop]
[add: Visits]
[if: hour >= 21 or hour < 7]
[tint: night]
[end]
[enter: pip center]
[if: Visits is 1]
A small round chick blinks at you from a basket by the window. This is Pip, and Pip is yours now.
Feed Pip, play with Pip, or just watch: if you choose nothing, Pip lives its own little life. Press A to let the days go by on their own, and J to see how Pip is doing.
[else if: time away > 1440]
[pip: happy]
[sound: chirp]
Pip spots you and bounces in circles, cheeping at the top of its voice. You were away for more than a day!
[add: Hunger 3]
[add: Mood 2]
[play: Bounds]
[else if: time away > 120]
[pip: happy]
[sound: chirp]
Pip hops to the edge of the basket to greet you.
[add: Hunger 2]
[add: Mood 1]
[play: Bounds]
[else]
Pip tilts its head, as if you had never left.
[end]
[play: Bounds]
[game mode]
[loop]
[if: Energy <= 2]
[pip: asleep]
Pip is fast asleep, a tiny puff rising and falling.
[else if: Hunger >= 7]
[pip: hungry]
Pip pecks at the empty bowl, then looks at you. Pointedly.
[else if: Mood <= 3]
[pip: sad]
Pip sits with its back to you, feathers ruffled. It will forgive you soon, with a little care.
[else if: Energy <= 4]
[pip: sleepy]
Pip's eyes keep closing on their own.
[else if: Mood >= 8]
[pip: happy]
Pip hums a tiny tune to itself.
[else]
[pip: normal]
Pip looks around the room, curious about everything.
[end]
[choice: Care 8s]
> Feed Pip = Feed
> Play together = Play
> Pet Pip = Pet
> Let Pip nap = Nap
> Just watch = Watch [timeout]
> Say goodbye for now = Bye
[if: Care is Bye]
[break]
[else if: Care is Feed]
[pip: eating]
[sound: munch]
[add: Hunger -4]
[add: Mood 1]
[play: Bounds]
Pip gobbles the seeds, crumbs everywhere.
[else if: Care is Play and Energy <= 2]
[sound: yawn]
Pip is too sleepy to play. It yawns at the ball.
[else if: Care is Play]
[pip: playing]
[sound: giggle]
[add: Mood 3]
[add: Energy -2]
[add: Hunger 1]
[play: Bounds]
You roll a little ball. Pip chases it, trips over it, and rolls after it.
[else if: Care is Pet]
[pip: happy]
[sound: chirp]
[add: Mood 2]
[play: Bounds]
Pip leans into your finger and closes its eyes.
[else if: Care is Nap]
[pip: asleep]
[sound: yawn]
[add: Energy 4]
[add: Hunger 1]
[play: Bounds]
Pip tucks its head under a wing. A short nap later, it is wide awake again.
[else]
[set: Pick = random 1 4]
[if: Pick is 1]
[add: Energy -1]
[play: Bounds]
Pip chases a speck of dust across the floor, very seriously.
[else if: Pick is 2]
[add: Mood 1]
[play: Bounds]
Pip pecks at its reflection in the window, and startles itself.
[else if: Pick is 3]
[add: Energy 1]
[play: Bounds]
Pip falls asleep mid-step, and wakes up confused.
[else]
[add: Mood 1]
[play: Bounds]
Pip sings a little song nobody taught it.
[end]
[add: Hunger 1]
[play: Bounds]
[if: Hunger >= 7]
[add: Mood -1]
[play: Bounds]
[end]
[end]
[end]
[pip: happy]
[sound: chirp]
Pip cheeps goodbye. It will be right here when you come back.
[ending: See you soon]
