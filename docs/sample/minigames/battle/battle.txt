# Battle
[bg: arena]
[music: battle]

[loop]

> Ripplet, the river axolotl: steady in everything = Ripplet

[enter: pyrrup 21% near facing-right slide-from-left]
[else if: Mine is Fernling]

[else]

[enter: ripplet 21% near facing-right slide-from-left]

[gauge: HP / {MaxHP} bottom right numbers = {Mine}]
[if: Mode is Duel]

[set: Trainer = Rei]

[set: Foe = Pyrrup]
[else if: Roll is 2]
[set: Foe = Fernling]
[else]
[set: Foe = Ripplet]
[end]
Rei, your old rival, steps into the ring with a grin.
[play: Fight]
[if: Result is Won]
[add: Battles won]
[sound: win]

[else]
[add: Battles lost]
[sound: lose]

[end]
[else]
[set: Potions = 3]
[set: Round = 0]
[loop]

[play: Next foe]
[play: Fight]
[if: Result is Lost]
[add: Battles lost]
[sound: lose]

[end]
[add: Battles won]
[if: Round >= 3]
[add: Gauntlets cleared]
[sound: win]

[break]
[end]
[sound: heal]
[add: HP 15]
[if: HP > {MaxHP}]
[set: HP = {MaxHP}]
[end]
[add: Potions]
A short rest between rounds: {Mine} gets some breath back, and the arena hands you a potion. Potions: {Potions}.
[end]
[end]
[gauge: none]
[if: Mine is Pyrrup]
[exit: pyrrup fade]
[else if: Mine is Fernling]
[exit: fernling fade]
[else]
[exit: ripplet fade]
[end]
[choice: Again]

> Leave the arena = No
[if: Again is No]
[break]
[end]
[end]
