# Blackjack
[bg: table]
[music: lounge]
[if: Money < 1]
[set: Money = 1000.00]
The house slides a fresh stack of chips your way: $1,000, on the house.
[end]
Welcome to the table. You have {Money} dollars to play with; the journal (J) keeps the count.
[choice: Rules]
> Read the rules first = Read
> I know how to play = Skip
[if: Rules is Read]
Get closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over. Number cards count their number, jacks, queens and kings 10, an ace 11, or 1 when 11 would take you over.
You and the dealer get two cards each; one of the dealer's stays face down. Hit to take a card, stand to keep your hand. Double down on your first two cards: double the bet, take exactly one more card.
The dealer draws until 17 or more. Beat the dealer and your bet is paid back twice over; an ace and a ten-card at once is a blackjack, paid 3 to 2. A tie gives your bet back.
[end]
[choice: Shoe]
> One deck = One
> A shoe of two decks = Two

> A shoe of eight decks = Eight
