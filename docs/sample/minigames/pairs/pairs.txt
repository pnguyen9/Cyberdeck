[import: story en]

[style: Ending = gold, large, fade-in]

[list: Deck = cat | cat | dog | dog | rabbit | rabbit | bear | bear | fox | fox | frog | frog | chick | chick | panda | panda]
[list: Shown = 16 × back]
[counter: Cell = 0]
[counter: First = 0]
[counter: Second = 0]
[counter: Wrong = 0]
[counter: Turns = 0]
[counter: Found = 0]
[counter lasting: Best = 0]

[shown: Turns, Found, Best]

# Pairs
[bg: meadow]
Sixteen tiles, eight pairs of animals. Turn two tiles at a time: a pair stays face up, two different animals turn back over at your next pick.
Find every pair in as few turns as you can. The journal (J) keeps your best.
[shuffle: Deck]
[loop]
[choice: Cell grid 4 × 4 from Shown with tile]
[if: Wrong is 1]
[set: Shown First = back]
[set: Shown Second = back]
[set: First = 0]
[set: Wrong = 0]
[end]
[if: Shown Cell is back]
[sound: turn]
[set: Shown Cell = {Deck Cell}]
[if: First is 0]
[set: First = {Cell}]
[else]
[add: Turns 1]
[if: Deck Cell is {Deck First}]
[sound: match]
[add: Found 1]
[set: First = 0]
[if: Found is 8]
[break]
[end]
[else]
[sound: miss]
[set: Second = {Cell}]
[set: Wrong = 1]
[end]
[end]
[end]
[end]
[sound: cheer]
Every pair found, in {Turns} turns!
[if: Best is 0 or Turns < {Best}]
[set: Best = {Turns}]
That is your best yet.
[end]
[ending: All pairs found]
