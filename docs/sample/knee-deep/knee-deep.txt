# Scene: First time

Mossbottom, a town of round doors, square meals and a great many dwarves, is getting ready for its Cheese Festival.
Or it was. Overnight, the Great Cheese Wheel, three hundred years old, as wide as a cart and twice as smelly, has rolled off its plinth, through the square, and down into the Delve.
[enter: mayor 72%]
— Mayor Puddifoot: Citizens! Our cheese has been cheese-napped! The tracks lead into the Delve, and the Delve, as everyone knows, is full of tunnels far too small for any reasonable person.
— Mayor Puddifoot: We need someone brave. Someone strong. Someone, and I say this with the deepest respect, knee-high.
[enter: dwarf 25% fade]
Every head in the square turns slowly towards you, the shortest dwarf in Mossbottom, who only came out to buy bread.
— Mayor Puddifoot: Splendid! A volunteer! What was your name again, small hero?
[ask: Hero = Pebble]
[set: Weapon = Rusty axe]
[set: Armour = Woolly jumper]
[set: Gold = 25]
[dwarf: scared]
— {Hero}: I didn't volunteer, I was just standing here.
— Mayor Puddifoot: That is how all the great ones start, {Hero}. Here is your grandfather's axe, which we found in a drawer, and twenty-five gold for the shops. Bring back the cheese!
[dwarf: think]
— {Hero}: Did my grandfather ever use this axe?
— Mayor Puddifoot: Once, on a stubborn turnip. The turnip won. Off you go!
[exit: mayor]
[dwarf: normal]
How to play: each room down there has doors, each with a clue to what lies behind it. Pick one, and deal with what you find. Fights are decided by rolling a twenty-sided die: roll high. If things go badly, the Rescue Gnomes will fetch you, for a fee. The journal (J) shows what you carry.
